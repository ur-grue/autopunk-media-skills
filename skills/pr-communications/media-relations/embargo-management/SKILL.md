# Embargo Management

## What This Skill Does
Produces a complete embargo management brief for a specific embargoed announcement, covering embargo terms, distribution protocol, breach response protocol with three distinct tiers, regulatory considerations, and a pre-lift checklist.

## When To Use This Skill
- You are distributing a press release or announcement under embargo and need a structured protocol for managing it
- You need to produce a copy-paste-ready embargo terms document to attach to embargoed material
- You want a breach protocol agreed internally before distributing embargoed material, so the team knows exactly what to do if someone publishes early
- You are unsure whether your announcement is an embargo, an exclusivity arrangement, or a hold — and need to choose the right approach

## What You Need To Provide
**Required:** An announcement summary (what is being embargoed — product launch, financial results, personnel change, partnership, research findings); the embargo lift date, time, and timezone; the distribution scope (who will receive the material — named journalists, a media list, wire services, analysts); the sensitivity level (routine / market-sensitive / legally significant / national security adjacent); the jurisdiction (where the announcement originates and where recipients are based).

**Optional:** Whether any outlet has been offered an exclusivity arrangement; whether the announcing organisation is publicly listed (triggers regulatory considerations); any previous embargo breaches with this media list; the spokesperson's availability window after the lift.

## How the Assistant Approaches This
1. **Drafts the embargo terms document.** A standalone, copy-paste-ready text that states the exact lift time with timezone, what is covered, what recipients may and may not do, whether the embargo is conditional, and a named contact for questions.

2. **Clarifies the arrangement type.** Defines embargo, exclusivity, and hold — then recommends which is appropriate for this announcement and why.

3. **Builds the distribution protocol.** Creates a tiered distribution list with delivery method, timeline, receipt tracking requirements, and exclusions (what not to include in the pack).

4. **Writes the breach protocol.** Three response tiers: minor/accidental (one outlet slightly early), major (full early publication), and hostile (an outlet that never agreed to the embargo publishes — a distribution error, not a breach). Includes internal escalation path and decision authority.

5. **Flags regulatory considerations.** Identifies whether the announcement may trigger Reg FD (US), MAR (EU), or FCA (UK) obligations. Frames as "check with legal/compliance," not as legal advice.

6. **Generates the pre-lift checklist.** A numbered checklist covering receipt confirmation, explicit agreement, spokesperson readiness, monitoring, and — where applicable — compliance sign-off.

## Output Format
Complete embargo management brief, 700–1,000 words. Six numbered sections: Embargo Terms Document (copy-paste ready), Embargo vs. Exclusivity vs. Hold, Distribution Protocol, Breach Protocol (three tiers), Regulatory Considerations, Pre-Lift Checklist. Professional register — written for a PR or communications professional managing media distribution. Practical and specific to the announcement described.

## Quality Criteria
- [ ] Embargo terms document is copy-paste ready — a PR professional could attach it to an email without editing
- [ ] Embargo, exclusivity, and hold are clearly distinguished with a recommendation for which to use
- [ ] Breach protocol has three distinct tiers: minor/accidental, major, and hostile (outlet never agreed)
- [ ] The hostile tier explicitly acknowledges that an outlet cannot breach an embargo it never accepted
- [ ] Regulatory considerations flag Reg FD, MAR, and FCA where relevant, framed as compliance questions rather than legal advice
- [ ] Pre-lift checklist requires explicit agreement from recipients (not assumed from silence)
- [ ] All times include timezone conversions where the distribution list spans multiple timezones

## Example

### Input

A mid-size technology company, MediCore Analytics, is announcing a strategic partnership with Alliance Health Group, a large hospital network operator. The partnership will deliver a new data analytics platform to 40 hospitals across England and Wales. Embargo lift: Tuesday 14 January 2025, 07:00 GMT. Distribution: 12 specialist health-tech journalists, 3 national business correspondents, 2 wire services (PA Media and Reuters). Sensitivity: routine (neither company is publicly listed). Jurisdiction: UK-based announcement with US media on the distribution list.

### Output

**EMBARGO MANAGEMENT BRIEF**

---

**1. Embargo Terms Document**

EMBARGOED UNTIL: Tuesday 14 January 2025, 07:00 GMT / 02:00 ET

The following material is provided under embargo and may not be published, broadcast, posted online, or shared on social media before the date and time stated above.

This embargo covers: the attached press release, all supporting images, the data sheet, and all quotes attributed to named spokespeople. Recipients may prepare stories, brief editors, and request interviews in advance of the lift time. Recipients may not seek third-party comment from Alliance Health Group or its hospital partners directly; interview requests should be routed through the contact below.

If any outlet publishes before the stated time, the embargo will be lifted immediately for all recipients.

For questions about these terms, contact: Sarah Langford, Head of Communications, MediCore Analytics. Email: sarah.langford@medicore-example.com. Mobile: +44 7700 900123.

---

**2. Embargo vs. Exclusivity vs. Hold**

An embargo is the correct arrangement for this announcement. The partnership affects multiple hospitals and will be covered by specialist health-tech media, national business desks, and wire services. No single outlet has a stronger claim to the story than another, and simultaneous publication ensures fair access. An exclusivity arrangement would risk alienating the specialist health-tech journalists who form the core audience for ongoing coverage. A hold arrangement is unnecessary because the launch date is fixed and confirmed.

---

**3. Distribution Protocol**

Tier 1 (Friday 10 January, 72 hours before lift): 12 specialist health-tech journalists. These reporters cover the sector daily and need time to arrange interviews and gather context. Material sent via secure email with read receipt requested. Each recipient receives an individual email (not BCC) with the embargo terms stated at the top of the message and repeated in the attached press release.

Tier 2 (Monday 13 January, 24 hours before lift): 3 national business correspondents and 2 wire services. Wire services receive the material closer to lift because their publication cycle is faster. National business correspondents receive a tailored top note highlighting the economic angle (40 hospitals, new technology jobs).

Receipt tracking: Sarah Langford maintains a distribution log. Any recipient who has not confirmed receipt within 4 hours of sending receives a follow-up call. No material is assumed received until confirmed.

Exclusions: Internal financial projections for the partnership, draft contract terms, and any quotes not yet approved by Alliance Health Group are withheld from the distribution pack.

---

**4. Breach Protocol**

Detection: Google Alerts for "MediCore" and "Alliance Health" are active from Friday 10 January. Social media monitoring covers X/Twitter, LinkedIn, and relevant health-tech forums. Sarah Langford is the single point of contact for any journalist reporting a suspected breach.

**Tier 1 — Minor/accidental:** If one outlet publishes within 30 minutes of lift (e.g. a timezone error causes publication at 06:35 GMT instead of 07:00), Sarah Langford contacts the outlet, notes the breach, and assesses traction. If the story is not being picked up elsewhere, the embargo holds for remaining recipients. If it is spreading, she escalates to Tier 2.

**Tier 2 — Major:** If an outlet publishes hours before lift or the story is circulating widely, Sarah Langford lifts the embargo immediately. She sends a single email to all recipients: "The embargo on the MediCore Analytics / Alliance Health Group announcement has been lifted effective immediately. You are free to publish. All supporting materials are attached." The spokesperson is notified and made available for immediate calls.

**Tier 3 — Hostile:** If an outlet not on the distribution list publishes the story, this is a leak, not a breach. An outlet cannot break an embargo it never accepted. Sarah Langford identifies the likely source (was the material forwarded? did a hospital partner disclose?). She assesses whether the unauthorised story is accurate and complete. If it is incomplete or misleading, she may choose to lift the embargo early so that accurate coverage can follow quickly. The distribution process is reviewed after the campaign.

Internal escalation: Sarah Langford has authority to lift the embargo for Tier 1 incidents. For Tier 2, she consults the CEO of MediCore Analytics, with a decision required within 15 minutes.

---

**5. Regulatory Considerations**

Neither MediCore Analytics nor Alliance Health Group is publicly listed. Reg FD (US), MAR (EU), and FCA (UK) obligations for listed companies do not apply. No regulatory information service filing is required. If either company's status changes before the lift date (e.g. an IPO filing), the embargo plan must be reviewed by legal counsel before distribution proceeds. US-based journalists on the list are subject to the same embargo terms; no additional US regulatory considerations apply for a private company announcement.

---

**6. Pre-Lift Checklist**

|---|------|------|
| 1 | All 17 recipients have confirmed receipt of the embargoed material. | Y / N |
| 2 | All 17 recipients have replied confirming agreement to the embargo terms. | Y / N |
| 3 | Embargo terms specify 07:00 GMT / 02:00 ET on Tuesday 14 January 2025. | Y / N |
| 4 | Press release approved by MediCore Analytics CEO and Alliance Health Group communications team. | Y / N |
| 5 | MediCore Analytics website queued to publish the announcement page at 07:00 GMT. | Y / N |
| 6 | LinkedIn and X/Twitter posts scheduled for 07:01 GMT. | Y / N |
| 7 | Dr James Chen (CEO, MediCore Analytics) briefed and available from 07:00 GMT for phone interviews. | Y / N |

## Known Limitations
- **Cannot assess regulatory obligations.** The skill flags potential triggers for Reg FD, MAR, and FCA rules, but determining whether a specific announcement falls under these regulations requires legal and compliance review.
- **Cannot enforce an embargo.** The skill produces the protocol. Humans execute it. No document prevents a journalist from publishing early.
- **Cannot predict which outlet will break.** Breach protocols prepare for the eventuality; they cannot prevent it.
- **Does not cover government or defence embargoes.** D-notice (DSMA-notice) procedures in the UK, classified information protocols, and government security embargoes are outside scope.
